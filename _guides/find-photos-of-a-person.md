A grandparent asks for photos of the baby. You want the pictures of your mother from the last trip for her
birthday. Ten years of a phone's camera is somewhere between five and fifty thousand photos, and the ones with
a particular person in them are scattered through all of it.

Grouping photos by face is the feature that solves this, and it is the feature that most often is not there
when you look for it.

## What already groups faces

- **Google Photos** groups faces well and lets you name them, but only for photos it has backed up. Face
  grouping is also switched off in some countries and regions, and when it is off there is nothing to turn on.
  The matching happens on Google's servers.
- **Samsung Gallery** and some other phone galleries group faces on the phone. Pixel's Photos app is Google
  Photos. Xiaomi's gallery has a People album in some regions and not in others.
- **Nothing else** on a typical phone does it. Files and most third-party galleries do not look at faces at all.

If face grouping is available to you and your photos are backed up, use it. If it is not, or your photos stay
on the phone, you need an app that does the grouping there.

## In PixOrg

PixOrg finds faces and groups them as part of reading your library. The models run on the phone. Nobody is
named until you name them.

1. Open PixOrg, tap **AI**, then **People**. Groups appear as the library is read, newest photos first.
   Anyone who appears in a few photos gets a tile.
2. Tap a tile to see that person's photos. Tap the name at the top to give them one: "Amma", "Maya".
3. From now on the name works in Search: "Maya at the beach", "Amma in 2024", "Maya and Ravi". Dates and
   places combine with it.
4. From any person's page, long-press photos to share them, make a movie of them, or move them into a folder.

![The People page in PixOrg](/assets/shots/04_people.jpg)

Grouping is never perfect, so there are three ways to correct it, all on the person's page:

- **Same as…** when one person has been split into two tiles. Pick the other tile and they become one.
- **Not Maya** when a photo in the group is someone else. Tick the photos, tap it, and those faces leave the
  group. You can also move them to another person, or make a new one.
- **Hide** for a person you do not want on the People page. The photos stay in the library.

Named people are never merged or dissolved by PixOrg on its own, so a correction you make stays made.

## Who gets left out

A few kinds of face are deliberately not grouped, because they would make a mess of the People page:

- Idols, statues, paintings and sketches. A shelf of god pictures is not a person.
- Faces in screenshots, receipts and other documents.
- Small faces, far-away faces and blurred faces. They are kept, so a search still finds the photo, but they
  do not form a person on their own. A crowd photo with many faces joins the people already known in it and
  never starts a new one.

Someone who appears in only one or two photos does not get a tile either. The page shows people who appear
often; the rest are reachable by search once named.

## Privacy

Face grouping is the most personal thing a gallery does, which is why it should not happen on someone else's
computer. In PixOrg the face models run on the phone, the groups and names live in PixOrg's own database
there, and nothing about a face is sent anywhere. There is no account to tie a name to. The free version
shows small ads under the grid; the ad network sees an advertising ID, never a photo, a face or a name.
