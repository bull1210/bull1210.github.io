A birthday is sixty photos nobody will scroll through in a chat. The same sixty photos as a one-minute
movie with the right song gets watched to the end, twice. Making one has always meant a video editor,
a template, a watermark unless you pay, and an evening.

The job is small: pick the photos, pick the order, pick a song, press go. Everything else is the editor
getting in the way.

## The usual ways

- **Google Photos** makes "highlight videos" on its own and lets you pick photos for one. The music is from
  its own library; your own song cannot be used, and the result is tied to the app.
- **CapCut, InShot and similar editors** can do anything, which is the problem. A simple photo movie takes
  twenty taps, the free version adds a watermark or an ending clip, and the app wants an account.
- **Phone galleries** (Samsung, Xiaomi) have a "Create movie" or "Highlights" option for a selected album.
  Worth trying first; the music choice is usually a fixed list.

If you want your own song, a specific order and no watermark, the general answers all cost something.

## In PixOrg

PixOrg makes the movie from a moment or any selection, with any song on your phone, in portrait for
sharing, and saves it to the gallery.

1. Pick the photos. Either open a moment under **Moments** and tap **Movie**, or long-press photos in any
   grid, tick the ones you want, and tap **Movie** in the action bar. Search results work too: search
   "Hampi 2024", tick, Movie.
2. Choose the **order**: By date, Newest first, As I picked, or Shuffle. The first photo in that order is
   the title card.
3. Choose the **music**. Pick one of the bundled tracks, or **Your music** to use any song or video
   soundtrack on the phone. Drag to where the song should start, so the movie begins at the chorus and not
   the intro. A short song loops; a long one is faded out at the end.
4. Tap **Make movie**. The sheet shows progress; the movie saves itself to **Movies/PixOrg** and opens
   when it is done. Share it from there like any video.

![PixOrg's movie sheet](/assets/shots/06_movie.jpg)

Details worth knowing:

- The movie is 1080 by 1920, portrait, 30 frames a second, the format every chat app and phone plays.
- Each photo appears once, with a slow zoom and drift, and fades into the next. The length follows the number of photos.
- The movie ends on a short "Made with PixOrg" card. You can leave it out by watching one short ad, or
  with PixOrg Pro. Everything else about movies is free, with no limit on how many you make.
- If you close the sheet while it is rendering, the movie still finishes and saves.

## Picking the photos well

A movie from a whole trip is better at thirty photos than at three hundred. Start from the moment, then
untick the bursts: PixOrg marks the best shot of each burst with a **Best** badge in every grid, so keep
those and drop the rest. If you have already [cleaned up the bursts](/guides/declutter-phone-photos/), the
moment is nearly ready as it is.

For a movie about one person, open them under **People** and pick from their photos, or search "Amma in
2025" and tick the results; the guide to [finding every photo of a person](/guides/find-photos-of-a-person/)
explains naming.

## Privacy

The movie is rendered on the phone, from your photos and your song, and written to your gallery. No photo
and no music file is uploaded. Nothing about the song leaves the phone either; PixOrg does not look up what
it is.
